# Aura Protocols — Project Context

## What This Is
A Next.js 16 **retail storefront** for research peptides (Aura Protocols LLC sells its own lot-tested compounds). Formerly an affiliate index — all vendor affiliate links were removed in the 2026-09-27 retail redesign. Old `/go/*` links 301 to our own product pages.

Live at **https://auraprotocols.com** (Vercel project `aura-protocols`; PAUSED until checkout ships). `shop.auraprotocols.com` 301s to the apex.

## Design System — "Pharmacopoeia" + storefront
- Scope class `.pharmacopoeia` (see `globals.css`). Tokens: paper `#EDE9E0`, paper-deep `#E2DCCC`, ink `#1C1A15`, ink-soft `#4A4438`, specimen `#A32B1F`, line `#C9C2AE`.
- Type: Newsreader display (`--font-newsreader`, italic accent word in specimen), Georgia body, JetBrains Mono micro-labels (`--font-jetbrains`, class `.s-micro`).
- **Square corners, 1px hairlines.** Storefront classes are prefixed `s-` and were ported from the approved mocks in the private repo (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-storefront-mocks/`). Port from mocks — never freestyle UI.
- Brand: `AuraLockup` (animated pulse-line A). Vial label art: `components/store/Vial.tsx`.
- **Vials are CRYSTAL-CLEAR glass — never frosted or milky** (Alvester, 2026-10-14 and again 2026-10-23). The glass stops live in `Vial.tsx` and are mirrored in `vial-3d.ts` (the turning vial) — change both together; any new vial art (emails, ads, mocks, gate scenes) uses the same clear glass.
- **A turning vial always comes to rest on its FRONT face, never on the QR code** (Alvester, 2026-10-18 and again 2026-10-23) — never pass `restOn="qr"` to `VialTurn`.

## Compliance rules (enforced)
- Research use only. No dosing, protocols, reconstitution, benefits, "studied for", or human-outcome language anywhere. Scientific names only; chemical-class categories; no "stack". Exception: a catalog `designation` (Wolverine, GLOW, KLOW for the three blends, 2026-10-10) is the blend's nickname. Since 2026-10-20 the scientific name leads everywhere, the vial label included (`vialName`: "BPC-157 /" over "TB-500"; three or four components two per line, drawn a size smaller past `VIAL_LINE_MAX`), and the nickname sits in the vial's small print in capitals (`vialParts`); it still names the kit box lid and search matches it. The six incretin/amylin products show their scientific names everywhere for now: their APro code names (APro-G1SM, G2TRZ, G3RT, CAG, CAGSM, RTCAG) are stored in `GLP_CODE_NAMES` (`data/glp-code-names.ts`, imported by no page, so they never reach the browser) and never shown until the processor clarifies (2026-10-10) — to show one, set it as that entry's `designation`.
- **Testing claim wording (2026-10-23):** the panel is **"up to a ten-point panel"** — always "up to", never "ten-point panel" alone, never "10X"/"7X". (The site's older "seven-point panel" copy, `SEVEN_TEST_PANEL`, predates this.) Lab line: "Testing includes ISO/IEC 17025-accredited US lab" — the lab is accredited, never the result. No NEW marketing (ads, emails, social, share cards) says "a certificate for every lot"; existing store spots stay. **Standard stamps (Alvester, 2026-10-23)** — the wording whenever an ad or email speaks to testing/accreditation (not required on every piece; one light testing mention per email): sticker **"Scan the vial" / "See its lab report"** (the QR opens that lot's matching certificate); headline **"Up to a *ten-point* panel"** + "Testing includes ISO/IEC 17025-accredited US lab." Use these, don't invent new taglines.
- `pnpm --filter @aura/shop build` runs `scripts/compliance-scan.mjs` and FAILS on banned phrases. Fix copy, don't widen the allowlist.
- Account gate: browsing needs an account; the sign-up agreement (21+, RUO, dispute policy) is recorded in `account_agreements` (`gate_attestations` kept for history). The gate fails closed (a failed status check shows it).
- Before unpausing Vercel: `RELEASE_CHECK=1 pnpm --filter @aura/shop test` must pass (every strength on the store has a live certified lot; no placeholder "Made by" process values — `MADE_BY_PLACEHOLDER` in `lib/catalog.ts`).
- **Before launch — Retatrutide / Cagrilintide 10 mg (`retatrutide-cagrilintide`)**: no supplier since Nana was dropped (2026-10-19); LKZ, Uther and Chengdu don't list it. Alvester hides it in Admin → Catalog & lots (owner-only; the Assistant can't) unless a supplier and a passing certificate are found first. Asking Chengdu/LKZ about a custom 5 + 5 mg blend.
